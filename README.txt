MARKETLY E-COMMERCE WEBSITE

Files:
- index.html: storefront structure, sample products, reviews, shopping bag and demo interactions
- style.css: responsive styling for desktop and mobile

How to run:
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open index.html in a modern web browser.

Notes:
- Product photos and Google Fonts are loaded online and need an internet connection.
- Products, prices, reviews and contact email are sample/demo content.
- Add-to-bag and wishlist controls are front-end demonstrations only. Connect a backend and payment provider for real orders.
